Our Story

A Sonoma Tradition

★ Serving Santa Rosa for over 40 years

The Bird Restaurant is a family-owned neighborhood restaurant where great food at a fair price, refreshing drinks, and live entertainment come together. Situated in Rincon Valley just minutes from downtown Santa Rosa and easily accessible from Highway 101, The Bird offers a welcoming destination for locals and visitors alike.

Formerly Willie Bird's on Santa Rosa Avenue, The Bird proudly continues a long-standing Sonoma County tradition of serving home cooked Thanksgiving dinner year-round.

We specialize in American cuisine made in-house from fresh salads, wood-fired pizza, our BIG BEEF burger, Friday slow-smoked BBQ, to our turkey soup. We also offer catering services for private events in our banquet room, outdoor patio or offsite at your event location.

The Bird also features a full-service bar with an extensive selection of beer, wine, and cocktails. Whether you're enjoying happy hour, celebrating with friends, or relaxing before a show, our bar is the perfect place to unwind.
